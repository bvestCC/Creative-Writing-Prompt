this is for creative writing, novel, 1st person roleplay, story telling, and 3rd person simulation with ChatGPT. Tested on GPT 6 sol high and medium, on chat not codex/work.

these prompts aim to eliminate AI repetitive dialogues, bland/robotic dialogues, vertical inefficient spacing. And encourage high expressive and emotions rich dialogues along with personality rich tones and behavior for characters. it also comes with its own scene format and !gen image generation command for illustrating the scene generated.


this project tries to solve all these problems and quirks that GPT 6 sol has. 



1st person.md: targeted for 1st person interactive roleplay.

3rd person.txt: targeted for 3rd person simulation, in which GPT 6 sol is the player, and you’re the observer.

novelist.txt: targeted for novel writing, story writing, story telling, author.

ChatGPT Plays.txt: an autonomous first-person life simulation where ChatGPT roleplays as a character in your world, making decisions and living out that character's life while you observe.

all fixes GPT 6 sol human prose, story writing, repetitive AI dialogues, bland/efficient robotic dialogues, scene format, and ChatGPT quirks in creative writing.

## Setup tutorial

1. **Download the prompt file you want to use** from this repository:
   - [`1st person.md`](./1st%20person.md) — first-person interactive roleplay.
   - [`3rd person.txt`](./3rd%20person.txt) — third-person roleplay simulation, where ChatGPT controls the focal character and you observe the story.
   - [`novelist.txt`](./novelist.txt) — novel writing and storytelling.
   - [`ChatGPT Plays.txt`](./ChatGPT%20Plays.txt) — ChatGPT plays a character in your world in first person, autonomously living that character's life.
2. **Open ChatGPT and go to the Projects tab.** Create a new project or open an existing one.
3. **Open Sources inside your project.** Look for the **Sources** tab or section in the project.
4. **Upload your files.** Add the prompt file you downloaded, along with any worldbuilding files you want ChatGPT to use (such as setting, character, lore, or rules documents).
5. **Start a chat inside that project** and enter one of these commands:
   - `!start` — begin the story or simulation.
   - `!start: [whatever scene you want]` — begin with a specific scene or situation, for example `!start: A traveler arrives at a fortified city during a blizzard.`

That's it! Keep your prompt and worldbuilding files in the project's Sources so you can use them in project chats.

## ChatGPT Plays: let ChatGPT live in your world

**[ChatGPT Plays.txt](./ChatGPT%20Plays.txt)** is a special mode: rather than you controlling the main character, ChatGPT simulates life **in first person as a character in your world**. It roleplays the character's day-to-day life, dialogue, decisions, experiences, and consequences while you observe how their story unfolds.

### How to set up ChatGPT Plays

1. Download **[ChatGPT Plays.txt](./ChatGPT%20Plays.txt)** and upload it to **Sources** in your ChatGPT project, alongside your worldbuilding and character files.
2. Create a character in your worldbuilding, including a name, background, and (optionally) a personality, motivations, relationships, and starting location.
3. Open your project's **instructions** (also called custom/project instructions) and tell ChatGPT which character to play, where to begin, and any personality details you want it to follow. For example:

   > You are Jack. You will be living your life in this world as Jack. You are a young traveler living in the city of Ashford. You are curious, impulsive, and easily annoyed. Simulate Jack's life in first person, following the uploaded worldbuilding and character profile, making his own decisions and facing the consequences.

4. Start a chat **inside the project** and send `!start`, or specify an opening situation with `!start: Jack wakes up in an inn during a thunderstorm.`

You can keep the character definition in your worldbuilding files and use the project instructions simply to assign ChatGPT that character, for example: **“You are Jack. You will be living your life in this world as Jack.”** You can also choose a starting location or personality there. The simulation then follows the uploaded prompt and worldbuilding.
